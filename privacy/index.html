Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications (listed below) developed by Elia Korkmaz for Apple Watch. We believe in privacy by design. Our business model is based on paid applications, not on selling your data.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Our applications are designed to be played offline on your Apple Watch. We do not require you to create an account, we do not track your location, and we do not collect usage data.

2. Kids Privacy

Many of our apps (such as Astro Numbers, Sudoku Zoo, etc.) are designed for families and children. These apps comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect any personal information from children.
