Privacy Policy

Water Clear: Speaker Water Eject

Last updated: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Elia Korkmaz ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Water Clear application for iPhone. We believe in privacy by design.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Water Clear is designed to work entirely offline on your device. The app plays audio tones through your device's speakers to help eject water. We do not require you to create an account, we do not track your location, and we do not collect any usage data or analytics.

2. Device Permissions

Water Clear may use the following device capabilities:

  • Speaker & Audio: The app generates audio tones played through your device's speakers to eject water. No audio is recorded or transmitted.
  • Haptic Feedback: The app uses the device's haptic engine to provide optional vibration feedback during water ejection. This is processed entirely on-device.

No data from these features is collected, stored, or shared with any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

Water Clear does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app contains no ads and does not communicate with any external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since our app does not collect any data from any user, it is safe for use by all ages.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
